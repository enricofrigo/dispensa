Always keep track of what you have in your kitchen, reduce food waste, and save time at the grocery store with Dispensa!

Dispensa is the free, ad-free, open-source assistant designed to help you organize your fridge, freezer, and pantry in seconds—on your own or synced across your whole household.

🚀 ADD ITEMS IN SECONDS
• Barcode Scanner: scan any barcode to automatically fetch product names and photos powered by Open Food Facts.
• Expiry Date OCR: scan expiration dates straight from the package label with your camera instead of typing them manually.
• Quick Consume Scanner: use the dedicated scanner to quickly decrease quantities as you finish products.

🔔 NEVER WASTE FOOD AGAIN
• Smart Reminders: get notified before your groceries expire.
• Opened Package Tracking: set shelf-life days after opening (e.g., milk to be consumed within 3 days of opening) and let the app recalculate the expiry date automatically.
• Clear Visual Status: color-coded cards make it effortless to spot fresh, expiring soon (yellow), or expired (red) items at a glance.

🗄️ TAILORED ORGANIZATION
• Customizable Locations: organize food across Pantry, Fridge, Freezer, Cellar, or create your own custom storage spots.
• Categories & Tags: label your items (e.g., Dairy, Organic, Gluten-Free) and filter or search them in an instant.

🛒 BUILT-IN SHOPPING LIST
• Out of an ingredient? Move it to your shopping list with a single tap.
• Check off items while shopping and share your list with anyone.

🔄 FAMILY SYNC (NO PROPRIETARY SERVERS)
Share one or multiple pantries across devices while staying in full control of where your data lives:
• Google Drive
• WebDAV servers (Nextcloud, ownCloud, etc.)
• Local Folder / Syncthing (SAF)
Invite family members in seconds simply by scanning an encrypted QR Code!

🔒 PRIVACY FIRST & 100% FREE
• Works completely offline: your data stays on your device.
• No mandatory accounts, no tracking, no ads, and no subscriptions.
• Full Backups: export and restore your database whenever you want.
• Supported languages: English, Italian, and German.
